
PROPOSAL : CAFE DELICE ROBERTSHAM - COMMUNITY CAFE 

1.Organization Overview

 NAME : Cafe Delice Robertsham
HISTORY: Family-owned neighborhood cafe and deli operating since 2022 on Kindon Rd, Robertsham, Johannesburg South. Offering fresh baked goods, halal sandwiches, and daily specials
MISSION: To provide affordable, fresh, homely food that brings the Robertsham community together daily
VISION: To be the most loved local cafe in Johannesburg South, known for quality and convenience.
TARGET AUDIENCE: Local families, parents from nearby schools, residents in Robertsham, La Rochelle, Turffontein
 Aged 25-55 looking for quick takeaway and dine in

2.Website Goals and Objectives

- Increase walk in traffic and online orders by 40% in about 3 months
- Provide accurate menu, prices, and opening hours
- Improve visibility on Google search 

3.Current Website Analysis

No website exists. 
STRENGTH: Loyal local customer base
WEAKNESSES: No menu online, no hours, customers cannot find location easily, losing online orders to competitors 
 IMPROVEMENT: Need mobile-first site with instant ordering

4. Proposed Website Features and Functionality

Homepage with daily specials slider
About Us story
Full Menu with prices and photos
Gallery
Contact page with embedded Google Map
Hours
Click-to-WhatsApp Order button
Instagram feed link

5. Design and User Experience

COLOR SCHEME : Cream , Coffee Brown , Pink, Charcoal 
TYPOGRAPHY : Headings: Playfair Display, Body: Aptos 11 (clean, readable as per guidelines).
LAYOUT: Single page scroll, sticky header with "Order Now, large food images, card layout for menu items
UX CONSIDERATIONS: 3 clicks to order, large tap targets for mobile, fast loading for users on data, halal badge clearly visible.

6. Technical Requirements

DOMAIN: cafedelicerobertsham.co.za
HOSTING: Hostinger or Afrihost (local, affordable)
CMS: WordPress/Wix for owner to update specials
 LANGUAGES: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, WhatsApp Business integration, Google Maps API
