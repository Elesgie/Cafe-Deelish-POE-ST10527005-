About Us

History

Family-owned neighborhood cafe and deli operating since 2022 on Kindon Rd, Robertsham, Johannesburg South. Offering fresh baked goods, halal sandwiches, and daily specials

Mission

To provide affordable, fresh, homely food that brings the Robertsham community together daily

Vision

To be the most loved local cafe in Johannesburg South, known for quality and convenience.

Target Audience

Local families, parents from nearby schools, residents in Robertsham, La Rochelle, Turffontein Aged 25-55 looking for quick takeaway and dine in
